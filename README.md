# Royal Wedding Invitation — Groom's Edition (Ashish & Shivangi)

An opulent, interactive digital Indian wedding invitation built with pure **HTML5, CSS3, and modern Vanilla JavaScript**. Designed for seamless mobile-first responsiveness, zero blurriness, and deployment on **GitHub Pages** and **Vercel**.

---

## 🎨 Multi-Theme Royal Palettes (Switchable Live)

The invitation features an interactive **Palace Theme Switcher** widget that updates the entire ambiance, gate lighting, cards, and countdown timer in real time:

1. **🌿 Mewar Emerald & Kundan Gold (Default Active)**
   - *Aesthetic:* Royal Rajputana Court, Imperial Forest Emerald Velvet (`#0a3a28`), 24K Polki & Kundan Gold (`#d4af37`), and Makrana Raw Silk Ivory (`#f8f6ee`).
   - The quintessential royal attire and jewel aesthetic for Indian grooms.

3. **🍷 Awadh Nawabi Jamuni (Lucknow Royal Court)**
   - *Aesthetic:* Deep Jamuni/Plum Velvet (`#340c42`), Antique Rose Gold, and Champagne Raw Silk.

4. **🌌 Sheesh Mahal Midnight (Royal Nocturne)**

## ✨ Interactive Features

- **3D Swiveling Palace Gate:** Dual-opening hand-carved lattice doors with perspective depth, swinging brass bells, and golden wax seal.
- **Cinematic Auto-Scroll:** Glides smoothly through the invitation post-opening at 60fps; auto-pauses on user touch or scroll.
- **Interactive Scratch-to-Reveal:** Digital 24K gold foil scratch card to unveil the sacred Vivah Muhurat.
- **Heirloom Countdown Timer:** Live countdown to November 26, 2026, 7:00 PM IST.
- **Direct WhatsApp RSVP & Share:** Instant confirmation with zero tedious forms.
- **One-Tap Navigation:** Google Maps directions to Umang Lawn, Lucknow.
- **Rose & Marigold Petal Shower:** Physics-based 60fps petal particle shower with toggle.
- **Royal Wedding Shehnai & Flute:** Background music with Web Audio synthesizer fallback.
